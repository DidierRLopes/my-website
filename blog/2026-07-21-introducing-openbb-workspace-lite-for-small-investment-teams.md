Today we're launching OpenBB Workspace Lite, a self-serve, self-hosted edition of OpenBB Workspace for small teams who want to run the same product in their own environment, without starting with enterprise overhead.

<!-- truncate -->

Investment teams rely on multiple data sources daily, feeds, internal models, portfolio data, and so on. But, so far, small teams lacked a right-sized solution to combine it all, distribute across colleagues and use AI agents on top.

If that's your team, you've been facing a bad set of options. You could build the software yourself, which takes considerable engineering resources until it finally pays off. You could bring your data and workflows into a rigid SaaS tool, and give up ownership and control in the process. Or you could try to justify software built for a much larger scale.

None of those tradeoffs make sense for a five or ten-person team, when that groundwork is already done, at a fraction of the cost.

In 2024 we released Workspace Pro. It solved the problem of data distribution, cross-asset and cross-source analytics and AI integration. But it was also built for firms that need enterprise infrastructure, SSO, and support across a broader self-hosted deployment.

Over time, we kept meeting another kind of team, family offices, boutique funds, emerging managers. Teams with a real need to distribute analytics AI workflows, who wanted a solution they could start tinkering with, not a massive commitment.

So today we're **launching OpenBB Workspace Lite**, a self-serve, self-hosted edition of OpenBB Workspace for small teams who want to run the same product in their own environment, without starting with enterprise overhead.

It is built for firms that want one place to work with their data and AI and share workflows across their team, on a smaller scale.

## What you get

With Workspace Lite, your team can deploy the OpenBB Workspace in your own environment and run a shared analytics surface on top of your data ecosystem. The product is **self-hosted** and built for **teams sharing workflows** rather than leaving them trapped in one person's notebook, spreadsheet, or local setup.

The core idea of OpenBB Workspace Lite is that your team's data, workflows, and agents live in an **environment you own**, not in someone else's cloud. There is no third party sitting between your team and your data, no vendor processing your workflows, and no dependency on the one person who happened to build the original dashboard.

For the onboarding, firms also get a **one-hour session with our team** where we can assist you with deployment, setup, data integration, or getting your first workflow live.

## Who it's for

Workspace Lite is for small investment teams that already have something to connect and need a shared way to work with it.

We believe it fits your firm if:

1. You already have some data in place, a database, a feed, an internal tool, portfolio data, or market data, and need a shared way to visualize it, analyze and distribute it.
2. Your team is passing work around through Excel, static dashboards, one-off scripts or Streamlit apps that are hard to maintain and lack AI capabilities.
3. You want to start using AI against your own workflows without scattering that work across disconnected, ungoverned tools.
4. Third-party solutions never seem to give you enough control to adapt them to your workflows. With Lite, you avoid the buy-vs-build trade-off, you get a workspace you can build directly on top of.
5. You don't need SSO or the scale that would point you toward the Pro Edition instead.

## The Lite vs. Pro distinction

Workspace Lite gives you the same core Workspace experience as Pro. The interface, workspace capabilities, data connectivity, and AI compatibility are the same.

What changes is the infrastructure layer. Lite is for teams that can manage access credentials manually, do not require multi-entity, cloud-native database and object storage infrastructure, or the support model that comes with Pro. Lite is also built for one team inside one company, not for reselling, white-labeling, or running it as a service for your own customers.

Even though it does not come with a strict seat limit, we expect most teams to outgrow Lite after 10 people. But ultimately it all comes down to your specific requirements.
